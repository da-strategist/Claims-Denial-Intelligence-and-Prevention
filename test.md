# Claims Denial Intelligence & Prevention Platform

A healthcare analytics platform that helps hospitals and health systems reduce revenue loss from claims denials.

## The Problem

Healthcare providers lose an average of 4.8% of net revenue to claims denials annually, with initial denial rates reaching 11.8% in 2024. The industry spends $19.7 billion per year fighting denied claims — over half of which is avoidable. 76% of denials stem from preventable data errors.

## What This Platform Does

The Claims Denial Intelligence & Prevention Platform provides a predictive analytics layer that sits on top of existing billing systems to:

- Classify denials by root cause, payer, procedure code, and department
- Surface denial risk scores before claim submission
- Deliver C-suite dashboards with KPIs, trends, and financial impact estimates
- Generate actionable recommendations for billing and coding teams

## Target Markets

US, UK, Canada, EU, and Middle East (GCC)

## Tech Stack

Synthea + CMS SynPUF (data), Python (ingestion), DuckDB/Snowflake (storage), dbt Core (transformation), Evidence.dev/Metabase (visualization), scikit-learn (ML scoring)
